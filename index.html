🌀 1. The Helix Kallol Doctrine

This doctrine proposes a spiral model for systemic change, particularly within digital ecosystems. It introduces five core operational models aimed at fostering decentralized, ethical governance:

  • YouTube University: A framework for a decentralized, video-based learning ecosystem that operates outside conventional academic structures.
  • Positive Staircase Model: A mechanism for structured growth and knowledge validation.
  • Helix Kallol Protocol: A method for establishing decentralized knowledge.
  • AEGIS & EMOT: Operational models within the doctrine designed for ethical AI engagement and community oversight.
🕉️ 2. Theoretical & Philosophical Frameworks

Chakrabarti has developed several frameworks bridging ancient philosophy with modern technology:

  • Sanatana Samyata: A concept exploring the balance between traditional knowledge (Sanatana) and modern, structured systems (Samyata).
  • AI and Buddhist Philosophy: A research area exploring impermanence, non-self, and ethics in machine learning systems.
  • The Paradox of Control: A study on Taoist teachings in relation to autonomy and responsibility in AI systems.
